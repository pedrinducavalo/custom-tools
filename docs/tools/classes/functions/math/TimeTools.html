@:value({ locale : "en-US" })staticformatDate(day:Int, month:Int, year:Int, locale:String = "en-US"):String

Formats a date using a common numeric date convention for the given locale.

Parameters:

day

The day of the month.

month

The month of the year.

year

The year.

locale

A locale tag such as en-US, pt-BR, de-DE, or ja-JP. Defaults to en-US.

Returns:

The date formatted as month/day/year for en-US, year/month/day for Japanese or Chinese locales (with slashes) and Korean locales (with dots), or day/month/year for other locales. German and Russian locales use dots as separators.
